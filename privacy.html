Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Zenta Business ("Zenta", "we") is a business record-keeping app for small businesses in Zambia. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, phone number and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase and is never visible to us).
  • Business records you enter: products, stock, sales, expenses, customer names and phone numbers, and Nkongole (credit) balances.
  • Subscription details: when your free trial started and until when you have paid.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the app for you, to show your reports, to manage your subscription, and to contact you about your account. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

Who can see your data

Your business records are visible only to you, when you are signed in to your account. As the operator, we can see your account status (email, phone number, trial and payment dates) so we can manage subscriptions. We do not use the admin screen to read your sales, stock or customer records.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in a data centre in South Africa, and is protected by sign-in and access rules so each business can only reach its own records.

Your customers' information

If you enter your own customers' names and phone numbers, you are responsible for having the right to keep that information and for using it properly.

Your choices

You can delete your account and all your records yourself in the app (More, then Delete my account), or ask us by email. Deleting your account is permanent.

Children

Zenta is meant for business owners and is not intended for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions or requests: zuluwebby150@gmail.com.
